# VActives Homepage Direction — Client Meeting, 29 August 2026

## Confirmed direction

- Keep the current hero visual and layout.
- Hero actions: `Start Hiring` and `Contact Us`.
- Replace the five-step story with only two numbered story sections:
  1. VActives is a recruitment agency.
  2. VActives continues to support the client after recruitment; the offer is not limited to candidate placement.
- Add `Know More About Us` directly after the two stories, linking to the About Us page.
- Add a `Who We Serve` section.
- Add a `Roles` section with three image-led cards for the three priority roles.
- Add `Know More About Roles` below the cards, linking to the About Us page unless the client changes the destination later.
- Add the hiring form near the bottom of the homepage as a clean, simple form block.
- Replace the current tall footer with a compact conventional three-column footer:
  - Left: logo and `Ready to build your remote team?`
  - Middle: `Services & Pricing`
  - Right: `Contact`
- Navigation wording must be `Services & Pricing`, not `Services`.
- Use real professional photography rather than 3D illustrations for the new visual direction.

## Pending content decisions

- Final wording and exact scope of post-recruitment support.
- The three priority roles and their approved descriptions.
- Final `Who We Serve` audience labels.
- Hiring-form fields and submission destination.
- Whether `Know More About Roles` should ultimately link to About Us or Services & Pricing.
